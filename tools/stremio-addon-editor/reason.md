### Why I didn't make script to generate logo ?

- Subject Separation is huge issue as it require background removal. It require large models to remove background which is not what a simple script for ease of use do.
- It is easier to do it online. Advised Tools :-
    - [Remove BG](https://www.remove.bg/upload) - Remove Background
    - [Boxy SVG](https://boxy-svg.com/app) - Final Formatting & Export
